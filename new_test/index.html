Welcome

Welcome to the wonderful world of 2D animation

In this introductory level course, students will be introduced to the wonderful world of 2D animation in Toon Boom Harmony. Harmony is the industry standard for animated content for films, televisions, streaming, commercials and in 2D game design.

Students will gain confidence as they experiment and develop skills in digital paperless animation through dynamic content and project-based learning. The course culminates with a certification exam. Digital animation combines traditional animation techniques and concepts with digital technologies, including software and hardware such as Toon Boom Harmony 22 and drawing tablets.

No previous animation experience is required. Additional resources are provided throughout the course in the form of links and lesson files.
