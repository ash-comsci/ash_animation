Welcome

Welcome to the wonderful world of 2D animation

In this introductory level course, students will be introduced to the wonderful world of 2D animation in Toon Boom Harmony. Harmony is used for animated content for film, television, streaming, commercials and 2D game design.

Students will gain confidence as they experiment and develop skills in digital paperless animation through dynamic content and project-based learning. Digital animation combines traditional animation techniques and concepts with digital technologies, including software and drawing tablets.

No previous animation experience is required. Use the Previous, Next and Jump to Lesson controls on every lesson page to move through the course.

Before you begin

Recommended setup & good habits

Keep your course work organized, take notes as you go and protect your project files with frequent saves and backups.

01

Software

Toon Boom Harmony Premium 22 or the version specified by your teacher.

02

Drawing Tablet

Wacom, Huion, XP-Pen or a similar pressure-sensitive drawing tablet.

03

Sketchbook

Keep a notebook nearby, take notes, add screenshots and date your entries.
