Lesson - 02 - The Animation Pipeline

About This Lesson

It is time to become familiar with the different animation workflows, how they end up merging together, and an overview of how they work with Toon Boom Harmony. An animation workflow refers to the steps and the order in which the different workflows occur when producing your movie or animated short. No matter which method you choose to create your project, the pipeline to follow will always be divided into three segments: pre-production, production, and post-production. You will then choose your animation method. Harmony is designed to handle part of the pre-production and all of the production while post-production is managed through an external application. The three workflow segments are broken down into different steps and will be presented in the Animation Pipeline presentation found below.
