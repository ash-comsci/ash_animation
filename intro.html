Welcome!

In this introductory level course, students will be introduced to the wonderful world of 2D animation in Toon Boom Harmony. Harmony is the industry standard for animated content for films, televisions, streaming, commercials and in 2D game design.

Students will gain confidence as they experiment and develop skills in the area of digital paperless animation under the guidance of dynamic content and project-based learning. This course culminates with a Certification exam.

Digital animation combines traditional animation techniques and concepts while using digital technologies, both software and hardware, such as Toon Boom Harmony 22, Wacom tablets, etc. Students do not require any previous animation experience to take this course. Please note that there are additional resources to enhance your educational experience. These can be found in the form of links and Lesson Files.

Recommended Hardware and Software

  • Toon Boom Harmony Premium 22
  • Drawing Tablet: Wacom Intuos/Cintiq, Huion, XP-Pen, etc.

Recommendations for Students

  • Keep a dated sketchbook/notebook for notes.
  • Create a Documents folder and work from there; save often (Cmd+S / Ctrl+S).
  • Back up to USB or cloud (copy & paste, never cut & paste).
  • Avoid working directly from USB to prevent corruption.
  • Only submit exported media files.
  • Recommended book: The Animator's Survival Kit
