Lesson - 01 - The World of Animation

About this Lesson:

It is time to start your journey into the wonderful world of animation. In this lesson you will learn about the history of animation and different animation techniques. The history of animation is rich with innovation, beginning in the nineteenth century and continuing right through into the present day. The earliest animation techniques employed optical toys, followed by the creation of animated films. The great cinematic achievements that we are familiar with today are the result of ongoing artistic and technological experimentation and discovery. Many of the nineteenth-century inventions designed to animate images were initially created as amusements for children. Most of these were optical toys that in time grew more sophisticated, resulting in a form of entertainment that proved popular with everyone.
