info About This Lesson
schedule Duration: 1 hour

Begin your journey into the wonderful world of animation. Discover the rich history of animation from 19th-century optical toys to modern cinematic achievements. Explore how ongoing artistic and technological experimentation has shaped the animation industry we know today.

Many of the nineteenth-century inventions designed to animate images began as children's amusements. These optical toys evolved into sophisticated entertainment that captivated audiences worldwide, laying the foundation for modern animation.
