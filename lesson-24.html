Lesson - 24 - Set the Stage

[video]

About

In this last lesson you will now need to apply all the skills you have developed throughout this course. You will be provided some creative freedom to design the elements to the Animated CNote character from the previous lesson.

Lesson Duration: 10 hours

Task:

  • Launch Toon Boom Harmony.
  • Open your Harmony file from the previous lesson. You will need to remove the existing background images
  • Take some time to investigate the construction of the Timeline layers and the Node View.
  • Before starting, you should review your notes from previous lessons.
  • Your final animation must be no less than the duration of the project file from the Support file.

Part 1 - Create or Add a Multilayer parallax layers:

You are to create the following in Photoshop, Illustrator or Harmony. This must be done as separate layers.

  • Layer - A Background Stage Design for CNote to perform his song.
  • Layer - An object to be placed in front of CNotes' position (Maybe a crowd, person, speaker, etc.)
  • Add these to your scene and position them along the Z axes to create some depth in your scene.
  • CNote should be positioned in between these two layers.

Part 2 - Add a Character:

You are to create the following in Photoshop, Illustrator or Harmony. This must be done as new layers.

  • This character must be positioned in the foreground of the scene.
  • Apply an Effect Node to the character.
  • This character must move across the Camera View as CNote plays his song

Part 3 - Apply depth and Camera Movement:

Follow the steps in the Video to complete the following:

  • Apply a Camera to your Scene.
  • Animate the camera effectively to help tell the story of your scene.

Part 4 - Apply Timing:

  • Apply Easing to your animation
  • Enhance the timing of your scene

Part 5 - Export:

  • Save your work.
  • Go to File/Export/Movie as Final Animation
  • Submit your animated scene
