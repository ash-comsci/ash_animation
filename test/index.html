Topics in this lesson

The source lesson groups the material into four major areas. Open each section to use it as a study guide.

01Persistence of Vision

Persistence of vision describes the way an image remains on the retina for a fraction of a second before it is replaced. This concept helps explain why a rapid sequence of still images can be perceived as continuous motion.

02Optical Toys

The Thaumatrope

An early optical toy that combines two images through rapid spinning.

The Zoetrope

A rotating device that creates the illusion of motion from sequential drawings.

The Flipbook

A sequence of drawings viewed rapidly by flipping pages.

The Praxinoscope

An optical animation device that improved on the zoetrope using mirrors.
